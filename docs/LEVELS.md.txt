# TAIFUN GAME — LEVEL STRUCTURE

---

# REGION 1 — CENTER

The first playable district of the game.

Visual style:
- bright daytime
- satirical bureaucracy atmosphere
- central city district
- clean readable mobile visuals

Gameplay style:
- exploration
- building encounters
- street events
- road repair gameplay

---

# OVERWORLD STRUCTURE

The player walks through the Center district.

The district contains:
- government buildings
- sidewalks
- road holes
- NPCs
- signs
- shops
- street decorations

The overworld is:
- compact
- readable
- mobile-friendly
- not open-world

---

# MAIN BUILDINGS

## 1. KURULTAI

Type:
- combat level

Gameplay:
- enter building
- defeat officials

Theme:

- bureaucracy satire

## 2. ADMINISTRATION

Type:
- combat level

Gameplay:
- harder enemies
- more guards
- denser encounters

Theme:
- stricter atmosphere

- more aggressive officials

---

# STREET GAMEPLAY

While exploring the district the player can:

- enter buildings
- interact with NPCs
- repair road holes
- trigger events

---

# ROAD HOLE SYSTEM

Road holes randomly appear in the district.

The player can:
- buy asphalt bags
- repair holes
- receive money
- increase city reputation

If the player stays offline:
- more holes appear
- the district becomes worse

---

# NPCS

Possible NPC types:
- angry citizens
- tired workers
- grandmothers
- taxi drivers
- street sellers

NPCs provide:
- humor
- atmosphere
- missions
- commentary

---

# DISTRICT PROGRESSION

As the player improves the district:
- fewer holes appear
- streets become cleaner
- NPC reactions improve
- visuals become brighter

---

# GAMEPLAY FLOW

Street exploration
→ enter building
→ defeat officials
→ receive reward
→ repair city
→ unlock next challenge
